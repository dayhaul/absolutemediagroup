About us

Absolute Media Group LLC is a limited liability company organized in the State of Texas and based in Austin. We design, build, and operate software products that solve everyday problems for the people who keep the economy moving — starting with America's professional truck drivers.

Our product

DayHaul AI

DayHaul AI is our flagship product: a daily planning assistant for professional truck drivers. It combines hours-of-service day planning, route-timed weather and road analysis, fuel-economy coaching, and shipper/receiver facility intelligence in one simple app.
